# R05: multiplicative relations among numbers alpha - zeta (zeta a root of unity)

Date 2026-11-22. Marks: [READ] read on the source page or PDF (through the fetch tool, which
returns a model digest of the page, except for Bass's PDF which I extracted with pdftotext myself),
[SNIPPET] seen only in a search result, abstract, zbMATH record or review, [INFERRED] my own
conclusion. Many endpoints throttled (see log); several PDFs were unreadable.

## 1. Summary

1. Classical case alpha = 1 [READ in Bass's own paper]: Bass (Nagoya 1966) proved Milnor's
   conjecture: all multiplicative relations, modulo torsion, among the numbers 1 - zeta are
   consequences of two families: the symmetry relation (1 - zeta^-1 = -zeta^-1 (1 - zeta)) and the
   distribution relation (product over eta^n = zeta of (1 - eta) = 1 - zeta). Ennola (1972) showed
   this is not literally all: there are extra relations ("Ennola relations") whose double is
   generated; the group of extra ones is of exponent 2; first example at conductor n = 105.
2. For fixed alpha != 1 I found NO paper that states multiplicative independence of
   {alpha - zeta : zeta root of unity} (or of 1 - zeta/alpha) for alpha an integer, sqrt(b) or
   b^(1/n). Searched: zbMATH, Crossref, arXiv abs pages, one WebSearch. This is "not found",
   weak evidence. The project's result for b >= 5 seems new as stated.
3. The nearest literature is the DUAL problem: fixed zeta, different integers: m - zeta_k versus
   n - zeta_k (Madritsch-Ziegler 2015 conjecture, proved independently by Drungilas-Dubickas,
   PAMS 147 (2019): independent for k >= 3 except (n,k)=(1,6)). Methods there: valuations of
   Phi_k(m) and prime ideals, not Baker.
4. Soft general results (non-effective): Laurent (1984, Lang's conjecture for tori) and
   Ostafe-Sha-Shparlinski-Zannier (Michigan Math J. 2019): for a FIXED finite tuple of rational
   functions, only finitely many points of K^ab give multiplicatively dependent values. They do not
   handle a tuple whose length grows with the order of zeta, so they do not give our statement.
5. Unlikely intersections (BMZ 1999, Maurin 2008, Habegger, BHMZ 2010) concern curves in G_m^n
   meeting algebraic subgroups. They apply to dependence of values phi_i(P) of fixed functions at
   points P of a curve. They do not apply directly to {alpha - zeta_j} (the torsion points vary,
   the map zeta -> alpha - zeta is not a homomorphism) [INFERRED].
6. Vanishing sums: Mann 1965, Conway-Jones 1976, Lam-Leung 2000, Dvornicich-Zannier 2000 (over a
   number field) are the standard tools; no new lead beyond what the project already uses.
7. Useful structural observation [INFERRED, my own derivation, check before use]: for
   |alpha| > 1 a relation prod (1 - zeta_j/alpha)^(e_j) = root of unity is equivalent (taking
   absolute values at all places / logarithms) to vanishing of a periodic-coefficient series
   sum_k rho(k) alpha^(-k)/k with rho(k) = sum_j e_j zeta_j^k (plus a conjugate-place system).
   This is exactly the shape of the project's series, so relations among 1 - zeta/alpha and BBP-type
   zero relations in the class with weights alpha^(-k) are the same question.
8. Top lead: write the project's b >= 5 argument for general real algebraic alpha (alpha = b^(1/n),
   |alpha| larger than some explicit bound), formal. See section 4.

## 2. What is known

### 2.1 alpha = 1: cyclotomic units

- [READ] Bass 1966 (Nagoya Math. J. 27, 401-407; PDF extracted by pdftotext). States an
  unpublished conjecture of Milnor: with e'(zeta) = 1 - zeta, zeta != 1 a complex root of unity,
  all multiplicative relations modulo torsion are consequences of (A): e'(zeta^-1) = -zeta^-1 e'(zeta)
  and (B): e'(zeta^n) = product over eta with eta^n = zeta of e'(eta). Theorem 1 is the precise form:
  write U_m additively, generated by symbols e(zeta), zeta^m = 1, zeta != 1, with defining relations
  (A)_m and (B)_m for n | m; then U_m modulo its torsion is the group of the real numbers
  1 - zeta modulo torsion (my reading of the garbled OCR text; the statement is "up to torsion").
  Also [READ]: the norm of 1 - zeta from Q(zeta_m) is 1 if m is not a prime power and p if m = p^a.
- [SNIPPET, WebSearch result and zbMATH] Ennola 1972, J. Number Theory 4, 236-247, "On relations
  between cyclotomic units" (Zbl 0236.12005; refs: Bass, Ramachandra). Ennola showed some relations
  are not consequences of (A),(B), but twice any relation is (so the discrepancy is 2-torsion).
  First example at n = 105 (from the Conrad/core.ac.uk snippet; the PDF itself returned 403,
  so [SNIPPET]). Kim-Ryu, Taiwanese J. Math. 18 (2014) 1653-1661, "A note on Ennola relation":
  torsion of the universal even punctured distribution is 2-torsion [SNIPPET]. Kucera, Acta Arith.
  174 (2016) 217-238: explicit construction of Ennola relations, bases of circular units and the
  Stickelberger ideal for composita of imaginary abelian fields each ramified at one prime
  [READ abstract on EuDML]. Conrad (J. Number Theory 81, 2000) "Construction of bases for the
  group of cyclotomic units" (title and DOI seen).
- [READ, Wikipedia "Cyclotomic unit"] For prime-power n the ratios (zeta^a - 1)/(zeta - 1) with
  gcd(a,n) = 1 generate the cyclotomic units; for n with two or more distinct prime factors the
  group they generate is in general not of finite index. Index of real cyclotomic units in the full
  unit group equals the class number h+ (prime power case; Sinnott for general n, Sinnott 1978/80/81,
  Kucera 1992 for bases; titles seen on zbMATH).
- Reference to look at next, not read: Washington, Introduction to Cyclotomic Fields, ch. 8 (units,
  Thm 8.3 for prime powers, Sinnott's index formula), Kubert-Lang "Modular Units" (distributions).
  I could not fetch Washington. So the statement "these generate all relations up to torsion and a
  factor 2" is established through Bass [READ] plus Ennola [SNIPPET]; its exact form (is it
  exponent 2 of the quotient, and for which n it is non-trivial) I did not verify in Ennola's text.
- Aside [SNIPPET]: arXiv 2301.04635 (determination of sets by their subset sums) uses "a delicate
  study of the structure of cyclotomic units" as the core; shows the topic is alive.

### 2.2 Shifted roots of unity, fixed alpha or fixed zeta

- [READ via ar5iv digest] Madritsch-Ziegler, "On multiplicatively independent bases in cyclotomic
  number fields" (arXiv 1408.3991; Theorem 2.2 there from their earlier paper): -m + zeta_k and
  -n + zeta_k are multiplicatively independent for m > n > C(k) (effective C(k)), and for all
  m > n > 1 when k is a power of 2,3,5,6,7,11,13,17,19,23; Conjecture: for all k. Theorem 2.3: if
  -m+zeta_k and -(m+a)+zeta_k are dependent (neither a root of unity), then the set S of primes
  dividing Phi_k(m) Phi_k(m+a) is a non-empty subset of the primes dividing a, with ord_k(p) <= nu_p(a),
  etc. Computation shows independence for 1 <= a <= 10^6. They quote BMZ 1999 (Remark 3): bounded
  height of m, but nothing uniform in k; explicit C_a = a^10 + 1 from Zannier's notes, much weaker.
- [SNIPPET, zbMATH review by H. Johnston] Drungilas-Dubickas, PAMS 147 (2019) 505-511: prove the
  Madritsch-Ziegler conjecture independently, via reciprocal algebraic numbers of degree >= 4 (and
  weaker statement for degree >= 6 with at least three conjugates in (-inf, 2]). For k >= 3 and
  m > n > 0, m - zeta_k and n - zeta_k independent except (n,k) = (1,6) [from the WebSearch result].
  The Madritsch-Surer-Ziegler 2017 paper also exists (no review text).
- [SNIPPET] Drungilas-Dubickas, Colloq. Math. 96 (2003): multiplicative dependence of shifted
  algebraic numbers (every quadratic number is Z-t-dependent, every cubic is Q-dependent; asks
  whether every algebraic number is Z_0-dependent). Dubickas 2026 (Axioms 15, 765) on shifted
  quadratic and cubic numbers: title only, MDPI page refused (403).
  Andersson, arXiv 1606.02524, "On questions of Cassels and Drungilas-Dubickas": conditional
  answer under conjectures about smooth numbers in polynomial sequences [READ abstract].
- [SNIPPET, zbMATH review] Habegger, "Multiplicative dependence and isolation II", J. London Math.
  Soc. 80 (2009): algebraic x with x and 1 - x multiplicatively dependent: height asymptotics to the
  Mahler measure of X+Y-1 (0.3230659...) and a count #M(D) ~ c0 D^3. This is the "curve x - y = 1"
  case: it concerns dependence between x and 1-x, not roots of unity.
- [SNIPPET] Dvornicich, "On an equation in cyclotomic numbers", Acta Arith. 98 (2001) 71-94,
  motivated by Schinzel's gcd of trinomials: uses Ennola's 1972 result; answer affirmative except one
  case (Q = 10). Shows Ennola's theorem is a working tool for equalities of values of cyclotomic-type
  expressions.
- Same family, different objects [SNIPPET]: multiplicative relations among Gauss and Jacobi sums
  (Yamamoto 1975 "gap group", Aoki 2013 "sign ambiguities of multiplicative relations between Gauss
  sums"): the relation group is again described by Stickelberger-type generators up to a 2-power gap.
  An analogue of Bass/Ennola; no overlap with alpha != 1 found.

### 2.3 General finiteness theorems

- [SNIPPET, zbMATH review by Beukers] Laurent 1984, Invent. Math. 78: Lang's conjecture for
  (C^*)^n: V intersect Gamma (Gamma finitely generated) is a finite union of cosets of subgroups
  intersected with Gamma; proof via Kummer theory and Evertse's S-unit sums. Not effective.
- [SNIPPET, review by Dubickas] Ostafe-Sha-Shparlinski-Zannier, Mich. Math. J. 68 (2019), arXiv
  1706.05874: for polynomials/rational functions phi_1..phi_n over a number field K that do not
  multiplicatively generate a power of a linear fractional function, only finitely many points of
  K^ab (maximal abelian extension) have multiplicatively dependent values. For iterates: alpha and
  phi^(n)(alpha) dependent for only finitely many n. Reviewer's text gives no effectiveness. Fixed
  tuple only [INFERRED].
- [SNIPPET, Waldschmidt's review via zbMATH] Bombieri-Masser-Zannier 1999 (IMRN): a curve in G_m^n,
  not in a translate of a proper subtorus: points on the curve lying in some proper algebraic
  subgroup have bounded height; dimension <= n-2 subgroups give finitely many points. Example
  quoted: for x + y = 1 the height of an admissible x is at most log 2. Proof: heights, geometry of
  numbers, Amoroso-David generalized Dobrowolski.
- Maurin, "Courbes algebriques et equations multiplicatives", Math. Ann. 341 (2008) [SNIPPET]:
  finiteness for codimension 2 in general; review mentions no effectiveness.
- Bombieri-Habegger-Masser-Zannier, "A note on Maurin's theorem" (2010) [SNIPPET]: three effective
  finiteness lemmas, "with the hope" of an effective version; i.e. Maurin's theorem is not known
  to be effective in general (as of that paper; I did not find a later effective version).
  BMZ 2008 (plane, Ann. Sc. Norm. Pisa) has the effective-friendly plane case [title only].
- Habegger, "Effective height upper bounds on algebraic tori" (arXiv 1201.3255; Autour de la
  conjecture de Zilber-Pink 2017) [READ abstract, SNIPPET review]: explicit height bounds for
  intersections of a subvariety of G_m^n with subgroups of complementary dimension. So height
  bounds are effective; finiteness (listing the points) is generally the problem.
- Evertse-Schlickewei-Schmidt, Ann. Math. 2002 [SNIPPET]: number of solutions of linear equations
  in a multiplicative group bounded by exp((6n)^(3n)(r+1)), uniform, but subspace-theorem based,
  ineffective as to location of the solutions.

### 2.4 Vanishing sums of roots of unity

- Mann 1965 (Mathematika 12) [SNIPPET: title]: structure of linear relations over Q among roots of
  unity with no vanishing subsum. Conway-Jones 1976 (Acta Arith. 30, 229-240) [SNIPPET]: complete
  tool for rational linear combinations of cosines of rational angles (used by Kanemitsu-Shirasaka
  1988 for tan equations and Kolpakov-Robins 2020). Lam-Leung, J. Algebra 224 (2000): lengths of
  vanishing sums of m-th roots of unity; in characteristic 0 the weight set is generated by the
  prime divisors of m [SNIPPET, from the review of the 1996 FFA paper; the 2000 review was not
  available]. Dvornicich-Zannier, Monatsh. Math. 129 (2000) [SNIPPET, Evertse review]: Mann's
  theorem over a number field L with bound on the prime-power part of the order in terms of [L:Q],
  and a rank bound for the L-span of {1, zeta_1, ..., zeta_{k-1}}. Also Dvornicich-Zannier 2002
  (sums vanishing mod a prime) and Zannier 1995 survey (applications to torsion points on curves).
  Christie-Dykema-Klep 2025 "Classifying minimal vanishing sums of roots of unity" [SNIPPET: title].
- Use for periodic sums [INFERRED]: for a periodic series sum rho(k) beta^(-k)/k with beta^2 = b the
  value is a combination of logs of the numbers 1 - zeta/beta; the minimal-length vanishing sums
  describe the "trivial" zero relations at beta = 1 (distribution relations), and Dvornicich-Zannier
  over the number field Q(beta) is the correct form when the coefficients are algebraic.

## 3. What is open

- O1. Whether, for every algebraic alpha that is not a root of unity, the numbers alpha - zeta
  (zeta over all roots of unity), modulo the "obvious" ones, are multiplicatively independent.
  Source: none states it; I found no statement as open or as proved ("not found" after: zbMATH
  searches on multiplicative dependence / cyclotomic / roots of unity / Schinzel / Dubickas,
  Crossref, arXiv abstract pages, one WebSearch).
- O2. Madritsch-Ziegler Conjecture 2.7 (independence of -m + zeta_k and -n + zeta_k for all k,
  m > n > 1): now a theorem (Drungilas-Dubickas) [SNIPPET].
- O3. Effective versions: Maurin's finiteness (codim 2 on curves) is stated not effective in
  general; a bounded-height statement is effective (BMZ 1999, Habegger 2017) [SNIPPET].
  Drungilas-Dubickas remark that BMZ alone gives nothing uniform in k [READ via ar5iv digest].
- O4. Ennola relations: complete description of the 2-torsion discrepancy for all n; done for the
  one-prime-per-factor compositum case by Kucera 2016 [SNIPPET of abstract]; whether it is fully
  settled for all n I did not determine.
- O5. Andersson's conditional answer to Cassels's question shows even the "easy" shifted-integer
  versions can depend on conjectures on smooth values of polynomials [READ abstract].

## 4. Leads for this project (ranked)

L1. General theorem for real alpha = b^(1/n) (days to 2 weeks).
  Statement to prove: let alpha > 1 be a real algebraic number with alpha^n = b a positive integer
  (b not a perfect power, or suitably normalised) and alpha >= alpha_0 explicit (for |alpha| > 2,
  alpha - zeta is never a unit-like small number; the b >= 5 proof for n = 2 already has
  alpha > 2.23). Then the numbers 1 - zeta/alpha (zeta over all roots of unity, together with -1) are
  multiplicatively independent. Why the toolkit fits [INFERRED]: (i) at primes above p | b every
  1 - zeta/alpha has the same valuation, so a relation has total exponent zero or the valuation
  condition kills it; (ii) at the archimedean places the logarithms are the periodic series
  sum_k rho(k) alpha^(-k)/k, the project's object; (iii) Baker's theorem is available. Also the
  trivial distribution product prod_{eta^n=zeta}(alpha - eta) = alpha^n - zeta = b - zeta ties the
  family of alpha to the family 1 - zeta/b, so for alpha = b^(1/n) the "obvious" relations connect
  different integers' families rather than lying inside one family [INFERRED]; a theorem should
  state this carefully (the independence for 1 - zeta/alpha itself should still hold when b >= some
  small bound). Worth noticing: this would be a clean statement not in the literature found, with
  the Madritsch-Ziegler / Drungilas-Dubickas theorem as its dual.

L2. Small alpha: complete classification of all relations among alpha - zeta for the cases where
  alpha lies in a cyclotomic field or |alpha| <= 2 (days). b = 2, 3 done; the analogues are
  alpha = golden ratio (phi = zeta_5-expression), alpha = sqrt(5)? (b = 5 is covered), alpha
  = 2cos(2 pi/k) (Salem/Pisot-type alpha with alpha - zeta a root of unity for specific zeta, e.g.
  alpha = zeta_k + zeta_k^-1 gives alpha - zeta_k = zeta_k^-1) [INFERRED]. Exact statement: for
  alpha = zeta_k + zeta_k^(-1) (k >= 5), the group generated by {alpha - zeta} mod torsion is free
  on ... (to compute, using prime ideals of Q(zeta_N); enumeration for N up to a few hundred on
  the 24-core machine). Worth noticing: connects Bass's theorem (alpha = 1) with alpha != 1 inside
  the same cyclotomic framework, so would be "Bass's theorem for a shifted base".

L3. Formalize the b >= 5 independence and the b = 2, 3 classifications as a standalone theorem
  "relations among 1 - zeta/sqrt(b)" and report it as a corollary (days; already machine-checked
  in the project). Why: the closest print results are the dual ones; a theorem with b >= 5 explicit
  and complete answers for b = 2,3 is a natural neighbour of Madritsch-Ziegler-Drungilas-Dubickas
  and would be publishable as a short note in the same journals (PAMS, Acta Arith., Lithuanian Math.
  J., Integers, Colloq. Math. appear as venues for this circle) [INFERRED from venues seen].

L4. Check relation of OSSZ (arXiv 1706.05874) to our setting (days, reading only). If a K^ab version
  of OSSZ holds with the tuple phi_i(x) = alpha - x^(k_i) for bounded exponents, it gives finiteness
  for bounded-width relations (relations involving at most r numbers alpha - zeta^(k_i), with r fixed)
  but not effective. Our approach could give an effective, uniform substitute, which is the stated
  weakness of Maurin/BMZ-based arguments [INFERRED]. Need to read their Theorem 1.1 and the proof;
  I only saw the abstract.

L5. Out of reach: general effective unlikely-intersection result on torsion points on the
  hypersurface prod (alpha - x_i)^(e_i) = 1 with unbounded number of variables. Laurent's theorem
  gives non-uniform finiteness of maximal torsion cosets for each fixed N only; uniformity in N and
  e is what is missing [INFERRED].

Remark on Ennola-type phenomena for alpha != 1 [INFERRED]: 2-torsion discrepancies arise from sign /
unit-root ambiguity; for a theorem "independent together with -1" as in the project, no such factor
appears because only a free-abelian statement is made. Still, for general alpha, state the theorem
modulo torsion of Q(alpha, zeta) to avoid the issue.

## 5. Overlap or conflict with the project's results (section 1 of the rules)

- No conflict found. Nothing found that states or contradicts: "for integers b >= 5 the numbers
  1 - zeta/sqrt(b) are multiplicatively independent together with -1" or the b = 2, 3
  classifications.
- Adjacent (not overlapping): Madritsch-Ziegler / Drungilas-Dubickas (fixed zeta, two integers
  shifted); Bass-Ennola (alpha = 1). The project's alpha = sqrt(b) results are of the "fixed shift,
  all roots of unity" type, the transpose of D-D's "fixed root of unity, varying integer".
- Mild caution: for b a perfect square (alpha integer) and b = 4, the distribution product
  (a - eta)(a + eta) = a^2 - zeta relates family a and family a^2; do not claim "no relations" across
  different integers [INFERRED]. The claim for a single family is not affected.
