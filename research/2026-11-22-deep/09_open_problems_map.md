# Open-problems map for one person plus an AI plus Lean 4 / Mathlib

Date checked: 2026-11-22. Tags: [READ] = seen in fetched content; [SNIPPET] = from a search-result snippet; "memory" = from memory, unverified.
Search budget: 2 of 5 WebSearch calls used. WebFetch: roughly 90 calls (a dozen failed with 401/403/404; see list).
Important access note: erdosproblems.com itself returned HTTP 403 to every fetch, so all Erdős data below comes from the public GitHub mirror (teorth/erdosproblems, google-deepmind/formal-conjectures) and from Tao's blog. mathoverflow.net and api.stackexchange.com are blocked for the fetch tool, and Sun's NJU home page refused the connection.

## Summary (10 lines)

1. The landscape changed sharply in 2026: AI systems (OpenAI internal models, GPT-5.x Pro, Claude, Aristotle, AlphaEvolve) now solve or improve many catalogued problems, so "easy" open items on public lists get taken within days. [READ: Erdős AI wiki, optimization-constants log]
2. Erdős problems: 1,221 problems, 593 "completely open", 8 "reduced to finite computation", 25 "falsifiable", 7 "verifiable" (GitHub statistics, [READ]). But on 6 Oct 2026 the site froze comments/proof claims, removed open/solved labels and credit language; results are now recorded by exposition, Lean proof and Palomar registration, not by "X proved". [READ]
3. Pi-adjacent: Ramanujan Machine conjectures are still being proven by outsiders via difference equations (38 proven in 2024; more in Jan-Apr 2026 arXiv notes). This is the best fit for a Lean-equipped hobbyist. [READ]
4. Zhi-Wei Sun keeps publishing 150+ conjectural series (arXiv 2603.29973, 31 Mar 2026); others prove them with WZ/Cauchy product/Taylor-coefficient methods within months. Provable-by-computer-algebra, verifiable in Lean. [READ]
5. Tao-Davis-Ivanisvili "optimization constants" repo: ~85 constants, 110 log entries in Sep-Oct 2026 alone, many AI-assisted; number-theory entries (Brun, Linnik, Turan, Schur-Siegel-Smyth, 45, 51, 82) accept certified improvements with citation and code. [READ]
6. Irrationality is hot and contested: Fauzan proved zeta(5) irrational (2026, as cited in formal-conjectures); Lai (2610.06838) and Fischler-Lai-Sprang-Zudilin (2611.16156) extend it; Sun's "Catalan's constant is irrational" (2609.04176) was challenged by Wachs (2609.22339), while formal-conjectures credits an OpenAI model. Professionals plus frontier AI are racing here; do not compete. [READ]
7. Formal-conjectures (DeepMind) has ~17 source directories and 1,021 Erdős statements; solved statements are credited by `research solved` plus `@[formal_proof ...]` with a Palomar URL, not by name. [READ]
8. Crowd Lean projects with claimable tasks today: IEANTN (Tao, explicit analytic number theory, "claim" bot), PrimeNumberTheoremAnd, Sphere Packing, FLT, Equational Theories. [READ]
9. Verification norm that "gets noticed": Lean proof registered on Palomar + short human-written exposition + arXiv note with honest AI disclosure. [READ: Bloom/Tao 6 Oct 2026]
10. Realistic best targets are finite computations, certified constant improvements, proofs of already-conjectured identities, and Lean formalizations of recent AI/human proofs; famous problems are hopeless.

## 1. Erdős problems (erdosproblems.com)

What it is: Thomas Bloom's database; Tao's GitHub mirror holds data/problems.yaml, the "source of truth". [READ]
Counts (STATISTICS.md, read 2026-11-22): 1,221 problems; 107 with prizes; proved 336, disproved 139, otherwise solved 101; completely open 593; open-but-reduced-to-finite-computation 8; falsifiable-by-finite-computation 25; verifiable 7; Lean statements 1,021; Lean solutions 318; 358 problems linked to OEIS, 310 potentially related to unlisted sequences (235 with no current link). [READ]
Status vocabulary: "falsifiable" (a finite counterexample would disprove), "verifiable" (a finite example would prove), "decidable" (both), "open". [READ, CONTRIBUTING.md]
AI contributions (the wiki, frozen 30 Jun 2026): categories 1a-1d primary (standalone, alongside literature, building on literature, human+AI), 2a-2d secondary (literature search, formalization, rewriting, computation). Examples: #1026 (Aristotle, Dec 2025, Lean; follows a Tao blog story), #333, #397, #205, #281, #125 (DeepMind prover, 30 Mar 2026, Lean), #38 (GPT-5.5 Pro, 25 Apr 2026), #90 (OpenAI internal model, 20 May 2026; Claude Mythos 26 May 2026), #42 (Harjas Sandhu with GPT-5.5 Pro), #283 (Kevin Barreto and Liam Price with GPT-5.5 Pro, 3 May 2026), #1196 (Nat Sothanaphan with GPT-5.4 Thinking, 16 Apr 2026; Tao et al. paper arXiv 2605.00301), #326 (Aron Bhalla with Aristotle/Codex/GPT-5.5, Lean). ## 3. Optimization constants (teorth/optimizationproblems)

What it is [READ]: curated table of best-known lower/upper bounds for about 85 constants; maintainers Damek Davis, Paata Ivanisvili, Terence Tao; submissions are reviewed only for "minimal standards of plausibility and replicability".
How to improve [READ, CONTRIBUTING.md]: new bound with citation; update the constant's page and README cell and the "Recent progress" log (stale README is the most common defect); value must be checkable by direct computation by a third party; code and certificates hosted elsewhere (DOI archive preferred), PR is Markdown only; AI use disclosed, AI-supplied references verified by the human. Bounds are kept as histories. Credit is by citation and chronological row; asterisk (*) marks unreplayed bounds and only a second party (referee, Palomar, maintainer, independent replay) can remove it.
Activity [READ]: 110 log entries; Sep-Oct 2026 saw many AI-assisted claims (Claude via D. Koshta on 49, 42, 86, 89, 1c; "Claude Opus 5.5" on 22b; OpenAI on 84a, 88a, 4b, 28, 65), R. Pinho on 42, 44, 68, 70, 82, 86, and independent replays by C. Toledo.
Number-theory / prime / sum constants (current table, [READ]): 7a irrationality measure of pi, upper < 7.101862832357 (Y. Bai, 10-11 Sep 2026); 7b Gamma(1/4) measure; 33 Ihara constant; 40a Lehmer Mahler measure; 42 Turan pure power sum, upper 0.6873358063* (Koshta with Claude, 23 Oct 2026); 45 density of odd integers that are prime + power of two, 0.107648 to 0.490180063290061 (a log note says the printed certificate is Griego's 0.490249407811155); 51 Erdős maximum-term, lower 0.5850788 (Sothanaphan, 1 Mar 2026), upper 2/pi; 56, 61, 62a, 62b, 63, 64 (exponent problems, professional-level); 65 Linnik, upper 5 with 2.4* and 3.99* in the log (conflict noted on the page); 66 Elliott-Halberstam; 72; 81 Brun's constant 1.840503 to 2.288513; 82 Zhang-Zagier essential minimum, upper 0.2534449* (R. ## 4. Ramanujan Machine

What [READ]: ramanujanmachine.com; algorithms (MITM-RF, ESMA) discovered conjectured continued fractions for pi, e, zeta(3), Catalan G, pi^2, zeta values, log 2; contributors can run searches, prove conjectures, or write algorithms, and new items are named after contributors. The results page says all zeta-order conjectures (2022) are unproven; for Apery's constant "two still unproven"; Catalan "a couple dozen found in May 2020, most still unproven"; pi^2 "about a dozen, most unproven". No community proof registry was readable (the community-results page returned 404).
Recent proofs by outsiders [READ, arXiv listings]: 2403.09729 proves 38 conjectures by solving linear difference equations and generalizes 31; 2601.08461 (identity for -pi/4), 2601.11892 (arctangent family), 2602.03027 (quartic continued fraction for 8/pi^2, "operator decoupling"), 2604.06239 (conjecture Z2 via Domb Apery-limit). 
Ramanujan Challenge for AI (arXiv 2607.09721; run 1 Jul-1 Aug 2026, now closed, results to be analysed): problems 2.1-2.8, ranked submissions: Lean/Rocq/Isabelle proof first, CAS derivation second, human proof third; submitter names and timestamps recorded; no prizes mentioned. [READ]
Conservative matrix fields: Leibtag, Barkan, Kalisch, Weinbaum, Shalyt, Kaminer, "New Directions in Irrationality" (2610.27799, 20 Oct 2026): improves mu(zeta(2)) < 4.62675, mu(zeta(3)) < 5.4197, proves 1, zeta(3), zeta(4) linearly independent. [READ]

## 6. Guillera

[READ, arXiv listing] Guillera's 1/pi^2 Ramanujan-like series were found experimentally; 1203.1255 (2012) says "modular type proofs" of the 1/pi^2 series had not been found (stale, 2012); 1610.04839 and 1910.01961 pose open conjectures (bilateral sums, p-adic expansions); 1908.05123 mixes proven and conjectured supercongruences; 2503.00570 (Mar 2025) gives WZ proofs of Au's seeds; 2604.04006 (Apr 2026) proves two Guillera-Zudilin supercongruences; 2602.06679 conjectures Fibonacci/Lucas supercongruences. ## 7. OEIS

[READ] OEIS style rules put only proven terms in Data and conjectural terms in Comments; the page does not say how proofs of conjectures are recorded (signed, dated comments are the norm); AI-generated OEIS submissions are forbidden (also stated in the Erdős-repo contributing file); there is a separate "Use of AI for OEIS Submissions" page that I did not read. JSON search worked only with plain queries (for example conjecture pi prime returned A20497, A720, A2375, A104272, A2372 with comments such as "purportedly" and proof notes). Sequences about the digits of pi: A60421 (k such that first k digits of pi form a prime), A65840. Erdős-OEIS link project (Tao, Aug 2025): 235 problems have no linked sequence yet. Useful because a computed, verified term extension is a checkable human contribution.

## Tempting but hopeless (one line each)

- Riemann hypothesis, Lindelof, Elliott-Halberstam: open for a century; bound improvements are professional only.
- Twin prime, Goldbach, Legendre, Cramer, Firoozbakht, Dubner: all open and formally recorded as open; only finite checks possible.
- Normality of pi, e, sqrt 2 (Borel): open since 1909; formal-conjectures lists it as open.
- Irrationality of e+pi, e*pi, pi^e, pi^pi, ln pi, gamma, zeta(7): open, Schanuel-type obstacles.
- Algebraic independence of e and pi; four exponentials; Schanuel's conjecture: open.
- Catalan's constant irrationality: contested (Sun preprint challenged, OpenAI claim); do not compete or rely on it.
- Collatz, Lehmer's Mahler measure, Wall-Sun-Sun, odd perfect numbers, abc: decades of attention.
- Rule 30 prize problems: open, formalized as prizes; very hard.
- Erdős problems with large prizes (for example #3, $5000): decades-old, no.
- Sphere packing, FLT, Carleson full formalization: team projects, not solo.
- Optimization constants 56-64 (Ramanujan exponents, Lindelof, divisor problems): professional analytic number theory.
