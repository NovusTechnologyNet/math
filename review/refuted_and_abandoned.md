# Refuted and abandoned

Claims that turned out false, guesses that failed, and approaches that cannot work, with the
reason. The rule is in `PRACTICES.md`, section 4: these are kept, and where possible the reason
is a Lean theorem. First written on 25 November 2026 from what the repository already recorded;
earlier dead ends that were never written down are not here.

| Date | What was claimed or tried | What happened | Evidence |
|---|---|---|---|
| 21 Nov 2026 | Our guess: the irrational multiples of pi in the 13-dimensional space of Theorem C come with sqrt 2, sqrt 3, sqrt 6 and two numbers of Q(zeta_20). | **Wrong** for sqrt 6: the third square root is sqrt 5. | `lean/PART3_PLAN.md` (M7, part 3); the five series are in `lean/Pilean/PiFormula/FiveSeries.lean` |
| 22 Nov 2026 | A printed list: 38737 is on OEIS A104885 (primes p with a binary BBP-type formula for log p); the entry comes from the list in Chamberland 2003. | **Refuted inside class F.** log 433 + log 38737 is a value of the class, and neither logarithm alone is. Our guess for the cause: 433 * 38737 divides the relevant cyclotomic value. Outside class F nothing is claimed. | `PiFormula.Unconditional.not_isFValue_log_38737`, `not_isFValue_log_433`, `PiFormula.isFValue_log_433_add_log_38737` |
| 22 Nov 2026 | The sub-series lemma of Part IV, as first stated for any base. | **False for perfect-power bases**: it fails for b = 9, n = 4 and for b = 8, n = 3. For b = a^j the right modulus is n1 / gcd(n1, j); Theorem L goes through the root a of the base. | `notes/pi_formula_theorem.md` (the remarks after the check of Theorem L) |
| 22 Nov 2026 | The expectation that, outside powers of 2 and 3, the logarithm of a root of the base is never a value of the step-2 class of the base. | **False for base 9**: log 3 = -2 log(1 - 1/3) + log(1 + 1/3) is a value of the class of base 9, because 3 - 1 and 3 + 1 are powers of 2. Statements about logarithms therefore go through the root of the base (Theorem M is not affected). | `notes/pi_formula_theorem.md` ("Base 9 is a true exception") |
| 22 Nov 2026 | Checking the certificate of Theorem J (26,204 nodes) with one `decide +kernel`. | **Abandoned**: it needs more than 22 GB of memory, because the kernel frees nothing until the declaration is finished. The certificate is now cut into closed pieces of at most 250 nodes, each checked on its own. | header of `lean/scripts/pairgen.py` |
| 23 Nov 2026 | Extending the parity argument for Erdos's conjecture from odd non-squares to odd squares. | **Cannot work.** The argument uses only that the values are odd (rationals with odd numerator and denominator), and for every odd square q > 1 there is such an f with sum 0. So for odd squares the conjecture needs another idea; it stays open from q = 11025 on. | `PiFormula.Erdos.exists_oddValued_sumIsZero_iff`, `not_cases_11025` |
| 23 Nov 2026 | A secondary source's exception list "n = 4, 6, 12" for Schinzel's theorem on primitive prime factors of the Aurifeuillian parts. | **Misattributed**: that list belongs to his Theorem 3, a different statement. For Theorem 2 with a = 2, b = 1 the exceptions are n = 4, 12, 20, as we had computed. | `research/2026-11-23-schinzel/02_paper_read.md` |
| 24 Nov 2026 | The assumption that every generated Lean file comes out of its script unchanged. | **False for two files**: `VSeries.lean` already differed from the output of `assemble.py`, and `Relations.lean` and `VSeries.lean` were rewrapped by hand. The other generated files were checked to reproduce byte for byte. | `lean/scripts/README.md` |
